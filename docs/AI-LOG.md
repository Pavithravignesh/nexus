| 2026-10-12 | Fix for Predictive insights, found from a production screenshot | The panel listed readings that already breached (CO2 1,778 ppm, O2 18.7 %) as "warning in ~0s". Cause: the simulator's injected spikes change `state.values` between reports while the stored reading status stays NORMAL, and the detector trusted that status. Fix: the detector scores the last value the device actually reported and derives its status itself, from the rounded value the browser sees (a second edge case: 29.96 °C rounds to the 30 °C bound). Added a regression test and a simulator-wide assertion that no flagged reading breaches; "<1s" replaces "~0s". |
| 2026-10-12 | Second fix for Predictive insights, again from a live screenshot | After the first fix went live, 5 of 6 rows were pressure readings "warning in ~25s": devices settling back to 1,013 hPa after a repaired high fault, with their fast-moving mean still at ~1,042, so the fall was extrapolated to the low bound (985). The last row was occupancy noise (5 vs 3 people) forecast to reach 40. Fix: score against a slow long-term normal (EWMA α 0.01) that a fault lasting minutes cannot redefine; treat a reading moving back toward normal as recovering; require 3σ (was 2.5σ) before trusting a forecast. Two regression tests reproduce both cases. Note: Vercel did not auto-deploy every `gh api` push; the first fix was deployed manually from the Vercel dashboard. |
